Application & Longevity Ritual

01

Pulse Points

Apply 2–3 spritzes directly to pulse points: the base of the neck, inner wrists, and behind the ears where body heat amplifies sillage.

02

Do Not Rub

Allow the perfume to dry naturally on the skin. Rubbing wrists together fractures delicate top notes and diminishes longevity.

03

Proper Storage

Keep your crystal bottle away from direct sunlight and humid bathrooms. Store in a cool, dark wardrobe to preserve the essences for years.
